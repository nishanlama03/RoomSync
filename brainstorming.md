## Favorite Existing Apps - Categorize and Evaluate
### Instagram
   - **Category:** Photo & Video / Social 
   - **Mobile:** Website is view only, uses camera, mobile first experience.
   - **Story:** Allows users to share their lives in pictures and enhance their content with filters
   - **Market:** Anyone that takes pictures could enjoy this app. Ability to follow and hashtag based on interests and categories allows users with unique interests to engage with relevant content.
   - **Habit:** Users can post throughout the day many times. Features like "Stories" encourage more candid posting as well. Users can explore endless pictures in any category imaginable whenever they want. Very habbit forming!
   - **Scope:** Instagram started out extremely narrow focused, just posting pics and viewing feeds. Has expanded to a somewhat larger scope including "Instagram Stories" (a la SnapChat) and messenger features. 
### Splitwize
   - **Category:** Finance / Budgeting
   - **Mobile:** Fully functional mobile app with a clean interface. Syncs seamlessly with the web version.
   - **Story:** Helps users track shared expenses and IOUs, ideal for roommates, friends, or travel groups. Solves the awkwardness of reminding others to pay you back.
   - **Market:** College students, roommates, travelers, couples, or anyone who regularly splits costs with others.
   - **Habit:** Habit-forming when used in ongoing shared financial contexts (e.g., monthly bills, group trips). Users tend to open it after a transaction or when settling up.
   - **Scope:** Narrow and focused on one main use—splitting expenses but has added features like recurring expenses, group settlements, and currency conversion.

### Life360
   - **Category:** Lifestyle / Family Safety
   - **Mobile:** Primarily mobile-focused with features like GPS tracking, driving reports, SOS alerts. Website mostly for admin/config.
   - **Story:** Gives peace of mind by allowing families to stay connected and share location in real-time.
   - **Market:** Families with children, caregivers, friend groups wanting to stay connected, or people in long-distance relationships.
   - **Habit:** Not used constantly, but provides passive value through background tracking. App is opened during coordination, emergencies, or location checks.
   - **Scope:** Started as just location sharing but has expanded into driver safety, crash detection, and even roadside assistance.

## New App Ideas - Evaluate and Categorize

1. RoommateSync

    - **Description**: A shared app for roommates to manage household tasks, shared expenses, bills, and communication. Includes features for chore rotation, reminders, payment history, and task delegation.

    - **Category**: Productivity / Lifestyle

    - **Mobile**: Mobile-first, since roommates often communicate or check chores/payments on the go. Could include notifications, push reminders, and camera/scanner features for bills/receipts.

    - **Story**: Helps roommates avoid miscommunication and passive-aggressive notes on the fridge. Keeps everyone organized and reduces tension over shared responsibilities.

    - **Market**: College students, young professionals, shared housing communities, co-living startups.

    - **Habit**: Used daily/weekly to track chores, add tasks, confirm bills, or settle expenses. Could become habit-forming if integrated with calendar notifications and real-time reminders.

    - **Scope**: V1 could support basic task assignment and payment splitting. V2 could integrate Google Calendar sync, notifications, and Venmo/PayPal. V3 could add chat and event planning. V4 might offer roommate compatibility scores or AI task balancing.

2. BookClub

    - **Description**: A digital space for book lovers to connect, form virtual book clubs, chat in real time, vote on what to read, and leave reviews.

    - **Category**: Education / Social / Literature

    - **Mobile**: Mobile is central for chats, updates, polls, and reading on the go. Could also support camera sharing for annotations or live discussions.

    - **Story**: Makes book clubs accessible and fun regardless of location. Encourages reading through community accountability and fun discussion prompts.

    - **Market**: Readers of all ages, students, remote friend groups, bookstagrammers, schools.

    - **Habit**: Weekly/daily check-ins for progress updates, discussions, voting, or sharing quotes. Builds habit through reading goals, scheduled meetups, and polls.

    - **Scope**: V1 could be private group creation + group chat + reading tracker. V2 could include polls for book selection and threaded discussions. V3 might support public clubs, event hosting, or author Q&As. V4 could integrate with Goodreads/Kindle for seamless tracking.

3. HouseMe

    - **Description**: An app that helps students and young professionals find housing or roommates that match based on lifestyle, schedule, cleanliness, and preferences.

    - **Category**: Housing / Lifestyle / Matchmaking

    - **Mobile**: Essential for messaging, matching, viewing listings, uploading pictures/videos, and real-time notifications for matches or updates.

    - **Story**: Reduces the stress and chaos of finding roommates and apartments. Prioritizes compatibility, safety, and transparency over random Craigslist posts.

    - **Market**: College students, recent grads, interns, young professionals, housing platforms.

    - **Habit**: Habit-forming during the apartment search period. Daily log-ins to view matches, chat with potential roommates, and check for new listings.

    - **Scope**: V1 could match based on lifestyle quiz + housing listings. V2 could add chat, document sharing (lease, utilities), and safety verifications. V3 could support roommate ratings, housing guides, and integration with campus housing. V4 might include partnerships with housing platforms or offer rent-splitting features like Splitwise.
